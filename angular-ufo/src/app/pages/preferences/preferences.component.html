How is the final score calculated?
  1. Start with your Raw score (each hit = +100; a miss costs 25, but raw never goes below 0).
  2. Divide by a time factor:
    • 60 s → ÷ 1
    • 120 s → ÷ 2
    • 180 s → ÷ 3
  3. Subtract 50 points for each selected UFO beyond the first: penalty = 50 × max(0, UFOs − 1).
  4. If Double speed was enabled, add +250 points.
  5. Clamp at the end: Final = max(0, …)
